Quick guide

How to make your wishlist

  1. 1 Pick your gifts Tap up to 8 gifts you'd like to receive.
  2. 2 Star your favorites Tap the ☆ on up to 4 gifts you want most.
  3. 3 Choose Want, Need, or Both Then tap a style and a budget. Add your size and color if you like.
  4. 4 Add your name and share Save the image or send it to the family on Messenger.

Edit your list?

You'll go back to your gifts. Everything you picked stays.
